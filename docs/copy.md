## Notes on wording

- Informal *du* throughout. "Gründer:innen" is used for gender-inclusive wording, and "jede:r Zehnte" in the same style.
- "Can imagine" (vorstellen) is kept separate from "will found" (gründen): scene 3 says so explicitly.
- The barrier percentages are multiple-choice answers, so they're never added up, and the screen says „Mehrfachnennungen möglich".
- The survey figures and the GEM figure are shown as separate facts with separate sources. No causal link is implied.
- The education-gap scene (optional in the brief) was left out because its figures could not be verified (see `docs/facts.md`).
